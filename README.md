# Miller & Bragg

**Interactive crystallography from Miller indices to X-ray diffraction.**

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23620323.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23620323)

**Use it online:** https://nockemann-lab.github.io/miller-and-bragg/

An interactive, single-file teaching tool for Miller indices and X-ray diffraction, for undergraduate and taught postgraduate courses in chemistry, materials science, physics and engineering.

- Draws the section of any lattice plane inside a unit cell and takes students through indexing one step at a time
- Generates matching exercises, question banks for Canvas and Moodle, and printable worksheets
- Covers tetragonal, orthorhombic and hexagonal (Miller–Bravais) cells
- Links planes to diffraction: a Bragg's law simulator with a simulated powder pattern, a powder-indexing exercise, and single-crystal diffraction with the Ewald construction and rotation photographs

## Usage

Download [`miller-and-bragg.html`](miller-and-bragg.html) and open it in any modern browser. It runs offline and needs no installation.
